01 / ABOUT

A little
background.

I am a doctoral student in philosophy at LMU Munich, supervised by Andrew Stephenson.

I completed my BPhil in Philosophy at Trinity College, University of Oxford in 2026, following a BA in Philosophy and Theology at Oxford. Before that, I studied theology at Heidelberg.

My BPhil thesis examines Kant’s justification of empirical cognition and the role of practical reason in that justification. More broadly, I am interested in the philosophy of mind and in philosophical questions about psychology and psychiatry.

Alongside my studies, my experience includes academic journal editing, teaching Ancient Greek, and student listening support.

Objective Validity and the Fact of Reason: Kant’s Justification of Empirical Cognition on Practical Grounds

Can practical reason help to justify our claims to empirical knowledge? My thesis develops a reading of Kant that connects the objective validity of cognition with consciousness of the moral law.
