About Research CV

I am a doctoral student in philosophy at LMU Munich, supervised by Andrew Stephenson. My research interests are in Kant, philosophy of mind, and philosophy of psychology and psychiatry.

I completed my BPhil in Philosophy at Trinity College, Oxford, in 2026, following a BA in Philosophy and Theology at Oxford. Before that, I studied theology at Heidelberg.

Research

My BPhil thesis examines Kant’s justification of empirical cognition and the role of practical reason in that justification.
