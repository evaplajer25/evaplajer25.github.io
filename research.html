Research

My main interests are in Kant, philosophy of mind, and philosophy of psychology and psychiatry. I also have interests in early modern philosophy, ethics, and epistemology.

BPhil thesis

Objective Validity and the Fact of Reason: Kant’s Justification of Empirical Cognition on Practical Grounds

University of Oxford, 2026

Thesis supervisor: Joseph Schear.

The thesis reconstructs a practical justification of empirical cognition in Kant. It argues that the Transcendental Deduction depends on the claim that the objects of cognition of all discursive beings instantiate the categories—a claim that cannot be established on theoretical grounds alone.

Drawing on Kant’s account of the moral law and shared practical objects, it develops an argument for accepting this claim on practical grounds. It then considers the consequences for empirical and moral scepticism, the primacy of practical reason, and the unity of reason.

The thesis is available on request.
